# Supply Agreement NWH-SUP-2026-014

**Between:** Northwind Hydraulics Sdn Bhd (Company No. 200901004472), of
Lot 12, Jalan Pengapit 15/19, Section 15, 40200 Shah Alam, Selangor,
Malaysia ("the Buyer")

**And:** Tanaka Seals & Gaskets KK, of 3-14-8 Nishi-Honmachi, Nishi-ku,
Osaka 550-0005, Japan ("the Supplier")

**Commencement:** 1 June 2026
**Term:** 24 months, expiring 31 May 2028

---

## 1. Scope of Supply

The Supplier shall supply fluoroelastomer (FKM) shaft seal sets for the
Buyer's NH-8840-X and NH-8860-X marine pump ranges, manufactured to the
Buyer's drawing DWG-8840X-SEAL-02 revision D, or such later revision as
the Buyer may notify under clause 9.

This Agreement does not cover nitrile (NBR) seal sets, which remain with
the Buyer's existing supplier.

## 2. Price

| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| FKM shaft seal set, DWG-8840X-SEAL-02 rev D | JPY 4,800 per set |

Price is fixed for the first 12 months. Thereafter either party may
request a review, supported by evidence of raw material movement of more
than 10%. Any revised price requires written agreement and takes effect no
earlier than 60 days after agreement.

Price is ex works Osaka. Freight and insurance to Shah Alam are for the
Buyer's account.

## 3. Minimum Order Quantity and Lead Time

- Minimum order quantity: **200 sets** per purchase order.
- Lead time: **6 weeks** from receipt of a valid purchase order.
- The Supplier shall hold a buffer of 100 sets from month 4 of the Term,
  callable by the Buyer at 5 working days' notice.

## 4. Payment Terms

Net **45 days** from the later of invoice date and receipt of goods at
Shah Alam. Payment in Japanese Yen by telegraphic transfer.

## 5. Late Delivery

Where the Supplier delivers later than the agreed date, the Buyer may
claim liquidated damages of **0.5% of the value of the delayed goods per
week or part week, capped at 5%** of that value. This remedy is without
prejudice to the Buyer's right to terminate under clause 11.

## 6. Quality Requirements

6.1 Every consignment shall be accompanied by a certificate of conformity
stating batch number, cure date, Shore A hardness at five points, and
compound identification.

6.2 Shore A hardness shall be **75 ±3**. Consignments outside this range
shall be rejected in whole.

6.3 The Supplier shall maintain ISO 9001 certification throughout the
Term and shall notify the Buyer within 10 days of any suspension.

6.4 Salt spray performance shall meet ISO 9227 for 720 hours on the
assembled seal.

6.5 Quality performance target: not more than 500 PPM defective, measured
quarterly.

## 7. First Article Approval

No seal set may be used in the Buyer's production until the Buyer's
Quality Manager has approved a first-article report covering three sets,
including dimensional verification, hardness at five points and the salt
spray test in clause 6.4. This is a condition precedent to any production
call-off.

## 8. Traceability and Shelf Life

Each set shall carry a batch number traceable to compound lot and cure
date. Shelf life is 24 months from cure date. Sets delivered with less
than 18 months' remaining shelf life may be rejected.

## 9. Engineering Changes

The Buyer may issue a revised drawing at any time. The Supplier shall
quote any resulting cost or lead time impact within 10 working days. No
change to material, compound, tooling or manufacturing location may be
made by the Supplier without the Buyer's prior written approval.

## 10. Warranty

The Supplier warrants each seal set against defect in material and
workmanship for **24 months from delivery**. The Supplier's liability for
a defective set is limited to replacement, plus the Buyer's direct cost of
removal and refitting where the defect is discovered after installation,
capped in aggregate at JPY 20,000,000 in any 12-month period.

## 11. Termination

Either party may terminate for material breach not remedied within 30 days
of written notice. The Buyer may terminate for convenience on 90 days'
written notice, in which case the Buyer shall take delivery of finished
goods already manufactured against firm orders.

## 12. Confidentiality

Drawings, specifications and pricing are confidential and survive
termination by 5 years.

## 13. Governing Law and Disputes

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Malaysia. Disputes shall be
referred to arbitration at the Asian International Arbitration Centre
(AIAC) in Kuala Lumpur, before a single arbitrator, in English.
