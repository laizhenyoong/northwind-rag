# Master Service Agreement NWH-MSA-2025-003

**Between:** Northwind Hydraulics Sdn Bhd ("the Supplier")
**And:** Pacific Marine Services Pte Ltd, of 8 Pandan Crescent, Singapore
128465 ("the Customer")

**Commencement:** 1 April 2025
**Term:** 36 months, expiring 31 March 2028

---

## 1. Scope

This Agreement governs the supply of hydraulic pumps, valves, spare parts
and related services by the Supplier to the Customer and to vessels under
the Customer's management. Individual purchase orders incorporate these
terms.

## 2. Products Covered

The NH-8800 and NH-8800-X pump ranges, the NH-2200 valve range, spare
parts, seal kits, filter elements, overhaul kits and adapter kits, together
with commissioning, overhaul, field service and training.

## 3. Pricing

3.1 Prices are those in the Supplier's current published price list, less
the discounts in clause 3.2.

3.2 The Customer receives a **10% discount** on pump list prices and a
**7% discount** on spare parts, applied before any volume discount.

3.3 The Supplier shall give **60 days' written notice** of any list price
increase. Orders acknowledged before the effective date are honoured at the
former price.

3.4 Prices exclude SST and are quoted FOB Port Klang unless otherwise
agreed in writing.

## 4. Payment

Net 45 days from invoice date. Overdue amounts carry interest at 1.5% per
month.

## 5. Warranty

### 5.1 Standard Industrial Products

The Supplier warrants industrial products against defects in material and
workmanship for **18 months from the date of delivery or 4,000 operating
hours, whichever occurs first**.

### 5.2 Marine Products

For marine-qualified products, including the NH-8840-X and NH-8860-X, the
warranty period is **24 months from the date of delivery or 5,000
operating hours, whichever occurs first**.

### 5.3 Remedy

The Supplier's obligation is limited, at its option, to repair or
replacement of the defective part, or credit of the invoiced value. The
Supplier is not liable for the Customer's cost of removal, reinstallation,
crane hire, vessel downtime, loss of hire or loss of profit.

### 5.4 Exclusions

The warranty does not apply where:

a. the product has been operated outside the published specification,
   including fluid viscosity, temperature, pressure, speed and cleanliness
   limits;
b. the prescribed maintenance has not been performed, including the
   **freshwater flushing interval for marine pumps in seawater-exposed
   service**;
c. filtration other than the specified element for the model has been
   fitted;
d. the product has been repaired or modified by anyone other than the
   Supplier or a party authorised in writing by the Supplier;
e. damage arises from incorrect electrical supply, including solenoid
   voltage outside the rated tolerance;
f. damage arises from transit, improper storage, accident or misuse.

### 5.5 Claims Procedure

Claims must be notified within 14 days of the Customer becoming aware of
the defect, with model, serial number, hour meter reading, a description
of the failure and, on request, a fluid sample. The Supplier may require
return of the part for assessment before accepting a claim.

## 6. Service Levels

| Priority | Definition | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Vessel immobilised or unable to sail | Attend within 24 hours |
| High | Operational restriction, vessel can sail | Attend within 72 hours |
| Standard | No operational restriction | Attend within 10 working days |
| Advisory | Technical enquiry | Written reply within 3 working days |

Response times run from the Supplier's written acknowledgement and are
measured to arrival on site or on board at a port within Peninsular
Malaysia or Singapore. Attendance elsewhere is by agreement.

## 7. Spare Parts Availability

The Supplier shall maintain spare parts availability for products supplied
under this Agreement for **10 years from the date of last delivery**, or
for the period stated in the Supplier's published end-of-life register
where that period is longer.

## 8. Liability

8.1 The Supplier's total aggregate liability under this Agreement is
capped at **RM 500,000** or the total value of orders placed in the
preceding 12 months, whichever is lower.

8.2 Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, including
loss of hire, loss of profit or loss of contract.

8.3 Nothing limits liability for death or personal injury caused by
negligence, or for fraud.

## 9. Insurance

The Supplier shall maintain public liability cover of not less than
RM 5,000,000 and product liability cover of not less than RM 5,000,000,
and shall provide certificates on request.

## 10. Confidentiality

Each party shall keep confidential the other's technical and commercial
information for 5 years after expiry.

## 11. Force Majeure

Neither party is liable for delay caused by an event beyond its reasonable
control. Where such an event continues for more than 90 days, either party
may terminate the affected order.

## 12. Termination

Either party may terminate for material breach not remedied within 30 days
of notice, or on 6 months' notice for convenience. Warranty obligations on
products already delivered survive termination.

## 13. Governing Law and Disputes

Governed by the laws of Malaysia. Disputes referred to arbitration at the
Asian International Arbitration Centre (AIAC) in Kuala Lumpur, before a
single arbitrator, in English.
