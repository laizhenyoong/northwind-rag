# Internal Acronyms and Terminology

Terms used in Northwind Hydraulics documents, forms and conversation.
Where an acronym has an industry meaning and a different internal meaning,
both are given.

## Company and Internal

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| NWH | Northwind Hydraulics Sdn Bhd. Used as the prefix on contract references. |
| SA | Shah Alam, the head office and factory site. |
| PG | Pasir Gudang, the service centre in Johor. |
| MC (1) | Management Committee, the weekly leadership meeting. |
| MC (2) | Medical certificate, in an HR or leave context. Context decides which. |
| HOD | Head of Department. |
| PC | Product Committee, which approves pricing and end-of-life decisions. |
| DR0 to DR4 | Design review gates, from requirements through to production release. |
| HTB | Hydraulic test bench. Rigs are numbered HTB-1 upward. |
| The register | Usually the end-of-life register, ENG-EOL-05. |
| The matrix | Usually the pump and valve compatibility matrix, ENG-COMPAT-11. |
| Float unit | A workshop-owned unit exchanged with a customer during overhaul. |
| Weepage | Slow seepage of fluid past a seal, short of a running leak. |

## Notes on Ambiguity

Two abbreviations cause regular confusion:

1. **MC** means Management Committee in a business context and medical
   certificate in an HR context.
2. **X suffix** on a model number always denotes the marine variant. It
   is never a revision marker. NH-8840 and NH-8840-X are different
   products with different pressure ratings, seal materials and service
   regimes.
