## 1. Welcome and Company Background

Welcome to Northwind Hydraulics Sdn Bhd. This handbook describes how we
work together. It is not a contract of employment, and where it conflicts
with your letter of appointment or with Malaysian law, those prevail.

Northwind Hydraulics was incorporated in 2009 in Shah Alam, Selangor. We
design and manufacture axial piston pumps, gear pumps and directional
control valves for industrial and marine hydraulic systems. Our customers
include shipyards, offshore service operators, palm oil mills, steel
processors and machine tool builders across Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand,
Indonesia and Vietnam.

We operate from two sites:

- **Shah Alam (head office and factory)** — design, machining, assembly,
  test, warehouse and all commercial functions.
- **Pasir Gudang (service centre)** — overhaul, field service dispatch and
  marine spares stock, opened in 2018 to serve the Johor shipyard cluster.

The Company employs approximately 180 people. Our registered company
number is 200901004472 and our SST registration is W10-1809-31000123.

### 1.1 Our Operating Principles

- **Nothing ships untested.** Every pump and valve is bench tested and
  certificated before despatch.
- **The isolation comes first.** No maintenance task begins before energy
  isolation is verified.
- **Say the number.** We prefer a measured figure to an opinion.
- **Own the callout.** Whoever takes the customer's call owns it until it
  is closed or formally handed over.

### 1.2 Organisation

The Company is led by the Chief Executive Officer, supported by the
Management Committee comprising the Heads of Engineering, Operations,
Sales, Supply Chain, Quality, Finance and Human Resources. The Management
Committee meets weekly on Monday mornings and formally reviews performance
monthly.

## 2. Employment Basics

### 2.1 Probation

New employees serve a probationary period of **3 months**. This may be
extended once, by up to a further 3 months, where performance requires
further assessment. Confirmation in writing from HR is required at the end
of probation. During probation, the notice period for either party is
**1 week**.

### 2.2 Employment Categories

- **Permanent** — open-ended appointment following successful probation.
- **Contract** — fixed-term appointment, renewable, stating an end date.
- **Apprentice** — enrolled on a structured scheme with a technical
  institute, typically 24 months.
- **Temporary** — engaged for a defined short-term need, normally under
  6 months.

### 2.3 Notice Periods After Confirmation

| Grade | Notice |
|---|---|
| Operator and Technician | 1 month |
| Executive and Engineer | 2 months |
| Manager | 2 months |
| Head of Department and above | 3 months |

Notice must be given in writing. Payment in lieu of notice is at the
Company's discretion.

### 2.4 Personal Records

Staff must notify HR within 14 days of any change to name, address, bank
account, marital status, dependants, emergency contact or professional
qualification. Statutory filings and insurance cover depend on these
records being accurate.

### 2.5 Employment of Relatives

Close relatives may be employed provided neither reports to the other,
directly or indirectly, and neither participates in decisions on the
other's pay, appraisal or promotion. Existing relationships must be
declared to HR.

## 3. Working Hours and Attendance

### 3.1 Standard Hours

Office functions work Monday to Friday, 08:30 to 17:30, with a one-hour
lunch break. The standard working week is 45 hours.

### 3.2 Shift Working

Production operates two shifts:

- **Shift A** — 07:00 to 15:30
- **Shift B** — 15:00 to 23:30

A 30-minute overlap allows handover. Shift allocation is published four
weeks in advance. A shift allowance is payable as set out in section 4.3.

### 3.3 Overtime

Overtime must be authorised in advance by the line manager. It is paid at
statutory rates: 1.5× on normal working days, 2.0× on rest days and 3.0×
on gazetted public holidays. Staff at Manager grade and above are not
eligible for overtime payment and instead take time off in lieu by
agreement.

### 3.4 Attendance Recording

All staff badge in and out at the turnstile. Failure to badge must be
corrected with the line manager on the same day. Repeated correction
requests are treated as an attendance matter.

### 3.5 Lateness

Arrival after 09:00 without prior notice is recorded as lateness. Three
instances in a calendar month trigger a documented conversation with the
line manager; six trigger a first written warning.

### 3.6 Absence Notification

An employee unable to attend work must notify their line manager
**before 09:30** on the first day of absence, by telephone or the HR app.
Notification by a colleague is not accepted except in an emergency.

## 4. Remuneration and Benefits

### 4.1 Salary Payment

Salaries are paid monthly by bank transfer, on the **25th** of the month,
or the preceding working day where the 25th falls on a weekend or public
holiday. Payslips are issued electronically through the HR portal.

### 4.2 Statutory Contributions

The Company deducts and remits EPF, SOCSO, EIS and PCB in accordance with
Malaysian law. Employer EPF contribution follows the statutory minimum
except for staff with more than 10 years' service, who receive an
additional 1% employer contribution.

### 4.3 Allowances

| Allowance | Amount | Eligibility |
|---|---|---|
| Shift allowance, Shift B | RM 12 per shift | Production, Shift B |
| Standby allowance | RM 60 per day | Field service on roster |
| Site allowance, offshore | RM 150 per day | Offshore assignment |
| Meal allowance, overtime | RM 15 | Overtime beyond 2 hours |
| Tool allowance | RM 300 per year | Technicians and fitters |
| Professional body fees | Up to RM 600 per year | Engineers, one body |

### 4.4 Annual Bonus

A discretionary bonus is considered annually, based on Company
performance against the financial plan and on individual appraisal rating.
Bonus is normally paid with February salary. Staff who have resigned or
are under notice at the payment date are not eligible.

### 4.5 Medical Benefit

Outpatient treatment at panel clinics is covered in full on presentation
of the staff card. Non-panel treatment is reimbursable up to RM 80 per
visit and RM 1,200 per year. Dependants are not covered for outpatient
treatment.

Group hospitalisation and surgical insurance covers the employee, spouse
and children up to age 21, with an annual limit of RM 60,000 per person.
Dental and optical are each reimbursable up to RM 400 per year for
confirmed staff.

### 4.6 Insurance

The Company maintains group term life cover of 36 months' basic salary and
group personal accident cover of 24 months' basic salary for all confirmed
staff. Cover ceases on the last day of employment.

### 4.7 Long Service Awards

| Completed service | Award |
|---|---|
| 5 years | RM 1,000 and a certificate |
| 10 years | RM 2,500 and 3 days' additional leave, one-off |
| 15 years | RM 5,000 and a commemorative item |
| 20 years | RM 8,000 and 5 days' additional leave, one-off |

### 4.8 Retirement

The retirement age is 60. Staff may request post-retirement engagement on
a contract basis, considered case by case and subject to a fitness
assessment for roles involving the factory floor.

## 5. Leave (Summary)

This section is a summary only. The authoritative document is the **Leave
Policy, POL-HR-002**, which prevails in the event of any difference.

Annual leave entitlement is 14, 18 or 21 days depending on completed
years of service. Up to 5 days may be carried forward and expire on
31 March. Paid medical leave is 14 days a year, with a medical certificate
required for absences of two or more consecutive days. Maternity leave is
98 days and paternity leave is 7 days.

Applications are made on form **HR-LV-05**.

During probation, annual leave accrues but may normally be taken only
with the agreement of the Head of Department.

## 6. Performance and Development

### 6.1 Appraisal Cycle

The performance year runs from 1 January to 31 December. Objectives are
set in January, reviewed at a mid-year conversation in July, and rated in
the annual appraisal completed during January of the following year.

Ratings are: **Outstanding**, **Exceeds**, **Meets**, **Partially Meets**,
**Does Not Meet**. A rating of Partially Meets or below requires a written
improvement plan with review points at 30, 60 and 90 days.

### 6.2 Training Budget

Each confirmed employee has an annual training budget of **RM 2,000**,
approved by the Head of Department. Training above this figure may be
approved by the Management Committee where it supports a business need.
HRD Corp claimable programmes are used wherever available.

### 6.3 Study Assistance and Bonding

Support for part-time qualifications is available at the Company's
discretion. Sponsorship above RM 10,000 carries a service bond, normally
2 years from completion, with pro-rated repayment if the employee resigns
within the bond period.

### 6.4 Mandatory Certifications

Certain roles require current certification, which the Company funds:

| Role | Certification | Validity |
|---|---|---|
| Maintenance technician | LOTO authorised person | 24 months |
| Forklift operator | CIDB competency | 36 months |
| Crane and lifting operator | DOSH competency | 24 months |
| Field service engineer, offshore | BOSIET | 48 months |
| Welder | Procedure qualification | 24 months |
| First aider | Occupational first aid | 24 months |

It is the employee's responsibility to notify their manager 90 days before
a certification lapses.

### 6.5 Internal Movement

Vacancies are advertised internally for 7 days before external posting.
Staff with 12 months in their current role may apply without their
manager's prior permission, though the manager is informed.

## 7. Conduct and Discipline

### 7.1 General Standards

Staff are expected to be honest, safe, courteous to colleagues and
customers, and careful with Company property. Behaviour that endangers
others, damages the Company's reputation or breaches the law is not
tolerated.

### 7.2 Dress and Personal Protective Equipment

Office staff dress smart-casual. Batik or corporate shirts are worn on
Fridays. On the factory floor and at customer sites the following are
mandatory at all times:

- Safety footwear with steel toe cap
- Safety glasses in machining and test areas
- Hearing protection in the test bay when a rig is running
- High-visibility vest in the warehouse and yard
- Gloves appropriate to the task, but never near rotating equipment

Loose clothing, jewellery, neckties and unsecured long hair are prohibited
near rotating machinery.

### 7.3 Alcohol and Drugs

Reporting for work impaired is gross misconduct. The Company may require
testing where there is reasonable cause, or after any incident involving
injury or significant property damage.

### 7.4 Gifts and Anti-Bribery

Gifts or hospitality from suppliers or customers with a value above
**RM 200** must be declared in the gifts register held by Finance. Cash,
cash equivalents and gifts during a live tender must always be refused.
Facilitation payments are prohibited without exception. Suspected bribery
must be reported under section 11.2.

### 7.5 Conflict of Interest

Any outside employment, directorship, shareholding or family interest in a
supplier, customer or competitor must be declared to HR in writing.
Approval is required before taking up outside paid work.

### 7.6 Confidentiality

Drawings, bills of material, test data, pricing, customer lists, supplier
terms and the contents of this handbook are confidential. Confidentiality
obligations continue after employment ends.

### 7.7 Harassment and Discrimination

The Company does not tolerate harassment, bullying or discrimination on
grounds of race, religion, gender, age, disability or nationality.
Complaints are handled under section 11 and may be raised directly with
the HR Manager, bypassing the line manager where appropriate.

### 7.8 Disciplinary Procedure

The normal sequence is: documented counselling, first written warning,
second written warning, final written warning, then dismissal. Warnings
remain active for 12 months.

For gross misconduct the Company may suspend an employee on full pay
pending a domestic inquiry. A domestic inquiry is convened with at least
2 working days' notice, the employee may be accompanied by a colleague,
and the panel comprises a chair, a member from a different department and
an HR representative.

### 7.9 Examples of Gross Misconduct

- Theft, fraud or deliberate falsification of records, including expense
  claims
- Bypassing or defeating a safety interlock or a lockout-tagout isolation
- Fighting or threatening violence
- Wilful damage to Company or customer property
- Disclosure of confidential information to a competitor
- Falsification of test or inspection results

## 8. Health, Safety and Environment

### 8.1 Shared Responsibility

Every employee must work safely, use the equipment provided correctly,
report hazards, and stop work that cannot be done safely. Stopping unsafe
work is always supported and never penalised.

### 8.2 Lockout-Tagout

Energy isolation is governed by **SOP-SAF-07**. Only certified operators
may apply or remove locks. The full six-step sequence is mandatory and no
step may be omitted, including the verification of zero energy.

### 8.3 Incident Reporting

All injuries, near-misses, spills and property damage must be reported to
the Safety Officer. Where a lockout-tagout failure or bypass is involved,
the report must be made **within 4 hours**.

### 8.4 First Aid and Emergency

First aid boxes are located at the test bay, the machine shop, the
warehouse and the main office reception. Trained first aiders are listed
on the notice board at each station.

On hearing the evacuation alarm, staff proceed to the assembly point in
the north car park and report to their department marshal. Lifts must not
be used. Do not re-enter until the Safety Officer or a fire officer
authorises it.

### 8.5 Environment

Waste oil, contaminated absorbents and used filters are scheduled waste
and must be placed only in the designated yellow bins in the yard for
licensed collection. Discharge of any oil to drains is prohibited and
carries statutory penalties in addition to disciplinary action.

### 8.6 Fitness for Duty

Staff taking medication that may cause drowsiness must inform their
manager before operating machinery or driving on Company business.

## 9. Information Technology and Data

### 9.1 Acceptable Use

Company IT is provided for business use. Limited personal use is tolerated
provided it does not interfere with work, consume significant bandwidth or
involve unlawful or offensive material.

### 9.2 Passwords and Access

Passwords must not be shared. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for
email, the ERP and the CAD vault. Access rights are granted on the
principle of least privilege and are reviewed twice a year.

### 9.3 Data Classification

| Class | Examples | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Brochures, website content | No restriction |
| Internal | Process documents, org charts | Staff only |
| Confidential | Drawings, pricing, contracts | Named recipients only |
| Restricted | Payroll, personal data, tender pricing | Encrypted, logged access |

Confidential and Restricted material may not be printed outside a Company
site, and may not be stored in personal cloud accounts.

### 9.4 Removable Media and Email

USB storage is blocked by default; exceptions require IT approval and an
encrypted device. Sending Company drawings or pricing to a personal email
address is a disciplinary matter.

### 9.5 Artificial Intelligence Tools

Public AI assistants may be used for general drafting and code assistance.
Customer names, drawings, test data, pricing and personal data must not be
entered into any public AI tool. Approved internal tools are listed on the
IT intranet page.

### 9.6 Personal Data

The Company processes personal data under the Personal Data Protection Act
2010. Staff handling customer or employee personal data must limit access
to what their role requires. Suspected data breaches must be reported to
IT and the HR Manager **within 24 hours** of discovery.

### 9.7 Monitoring

The Company may monitor its systems for security and compliance purposes,
proportionately and in accordance with law.

## 10. Travel (Summary)

This section is a summary. The authoritative document is the **Travel and
Expense Policy, POL-FIN-004**, currently at version 2.1 effective
1 March 2026, which prevails in the event of any difference.

Per diem is RM 180 domestic and RM 420 international, with Singapore and
Thailand capped at RM 300. Claims below RM 500 require line manager
approval; RM 500 and above require the Finance Director. All claims use
form **FIN-EXP-22** and must be submitted within 30 days of return.
Economy class is the default, with business class available only for
flights over 7 hours at Director grade and above.

Passport and visa costs for business travel are reimbursed. Personal
travel appended to a business trip is at the employee's own cost and must
not increase the fare paid by the Company.

## 11. Grievances and Whistleblowing

### 11.1 Grievance Procedure

Raise the matter first with your line manager, who responds within 5
working days. If unresolved, or if the grievance concerns the line
manager, escalate in writing to the Head of Department, who responds
within 10 working days. Final appeal is to the HR Manager, whose decision
concludes the internal process.

### 11.2 Whistleblowing

Concerns about fraud, bribery, safety falsification, environmental breach
or any unlawful act may be reported confidentially to the Chair of the
Audit Committee at `speakup@northwindhydraulics.example`. Reports may be
made anonymously. The Company prohibits retaliation against anyone
raising a concern in good faith; retaliation is itself gross misconduct.

## 12. Leaving the Company

### 12.1 Resignation

Submit written notice to your line manager, copied to HR, observing the
notice period in section 2.3.

### 12.2 Exit Process

Before the last working day the employee must return the laptop, staff
card, keys, tools, PPE, uniforms and any Company documents; complete
handover notes; and settle staff loans and outstanding expense claims. HR
conducts an exit interview, which is confidential and does not form part
of the reference.

### 12.3 Final Settlement

Final salary, payment for unused annual leave and any deductions are
settled in the payroll run following the last working day. Tax clearance
is arranged by Finance and may delay release of the final payment.

### 12.4 References

The Company provides factual references only, confirming role, dates of
employment and reason for leaving. Requests must be directed to HR;
individual managers must not issue references on Company letterhead.

## 13. Facilities and Site Rules

### 13.1 Access

Staff cards are personal. Allowing another person to use your card, or
tailgating through the turnstile, is a disciplinary matter. Lost cards must
be reported to Security immediately; a replacement costs RM 25.

### 13.2 Visitors

All visitors sign in at reception, wear a visitor badge, receive a safety
briefing before entering the factory, and are escorted at all times. The
host is responsible for the visitor.

### 13.3 Parking

Parking is available in the north and east car parks on a first-come
basis. Bays marked for visitors, disabled access, fire access or
loading must never be used by staff.

### 13.4 Canteen and Prayer Room

The canteen operates 07:00 to 20:00. The surau is on the first floor of
the office block, with ablution facilities adjacent.

### 13.5 Photography

Photography in the factory, test bay and CAD area requires written
permission from the Head of Department. Customer equipment must never be
photographed without the customer's consent.

### 13.6 Smoking

Smoking and vaping are permitted only in the designated shelter beside the
east car park. Smoking anywhere in the factory, warehouse, test bay or
yard is gross misconduct owing to the presence of hydraulic fluid and
solvents.
