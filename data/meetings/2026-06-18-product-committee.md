## 1. NH-8840-X Pricing Decision

Referred from the supply chain review of 3 May, following a 22% increase
in aluminium bronze billet cost adding approximately RM 980 to housing
material cost, and the higher unit price of the Tanaka FKM seal.

Priya Ramasamy presented margin analysis. At the existing list price of
**RM 17,500** the NH-8840-X margin had fallen to 24.8%, against a target
of 30% for marine product.

Options considered:

1. Hold price and accept the margin — rejected, as marine is the growth
   segment and would drag the blended margin below plan.
2. Increase to RM 18,400, restoring margin to 28.6% — considered
   insufficient.
3. Increase to RM 18,900, restoring margin to 30.1% — **approved.**
4. Increase to RM 19,500 — rejected as it would exceed the nearest
   competitor's list price.

**Decision:** the NH-8840-X list price is increased from RM 17,500 to
**RM 18,900 with effect from 1 July 2026**, an increase of 8.0%.

Transitional arrangements:

- Quotations issued before 18 June remain valid at RM 17,500 for their
  stated validity period, normally 30 days.
- Orders already acknowledged are honoured at the old price.
- The NH-8860-X is increased from RM 22,600 to RM 24,300 on the same date
  and for the same reason.

**Action:** Tan Mei Ling to publish price list 2026.2 effective 1 July and
notify all distributors 14 days in advance. *(Owner: TML, due 20 June)*

## 2. NH-4420 Discontinuation

Chong Wei Ming proposed formal withdrawal of the NH-4420 marine pump. The
model has been superseded in all respects by the NH-8840-X, which holds
current DNV type approval; the NH-4420's approval lapsed in 2024 and would
require costly re-certification to maintain.

Volumes: 6 units in FY2025 and 2 units in FY2026 to date.

**Decision:** the NH-4420 is discontinued with effect from **30 June
2026**. The replacement is the NH-8840-X.

Spares commitment: given typical vessel service life, spares will be
supported for **five years, to 30 June 2031**. This is longer than the
standard commitment and was approved specifically for this model.

Noted again that the NH-8840-X is not a drop-in replacement, owing to the
30 mm parallel-key versus 32 mm splined shaft difference. Vincent Teoh's
retrofit note is now issued to Sales.

**Action:** Vincent Teoh to write to all fourteen NH-4420 operators
confirming the withdrawal date and the five-year spares commitment.
*(Owner: VT, due 30 June)*

## 3. NH-2210 Coil Voltage Enquiries

Vincent Teoh reported a recurring pattern of customers requesting a 12 V
coil for the NH-2210, and two field incidents where a 12 V supply was
connected to a 24 V coil, causing incomplete spool shift that was
initially reported as a valve fault.

The Committee reaffirmed that no 12 V coil is offered on the NH-2200
series and that CB-2210-12 was withdrawn at the end of 2022.

**Action:** Vincent Teoh to add a bold coil voltage warning to the
commissioning checklist and to the quotation template. *(Owner: VT, due
15 July)*

## 4. Marine Backlog Status

Siti Zubaidah reported the marine backlog has fallen from eleven units at
the end of Q1 to four units, as conforming seal stock was released. Full
normalisation depends on Tanaka first-article approval, which Rajesh Nair
expects to complete in early June.

## 5. Adapter Kit Attach Rate

Vincent Teoh raised that only 60% of NH-8840-X orders that also include an
NG10 directional valve carry the required ADK-221-M adapter kit, meaning
roughly four in ten such orders will arrive incomplete and generate a
support ticket.

**Decision:** the quotation system will be configured to add ADK-221-M
automatically whenever an NH-8840-X and an NH-2210 or NH-2215 appear on
the same quotation, with an explicit override required to remove it.

**Action:** Tan Mei Ling to raise the change request with IT. *(Owner:
TML, due 15 July)*
