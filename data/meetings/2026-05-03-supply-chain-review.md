## 1. Marine Seal Vendor Changeover — Commercial Terms

Following the engineering sync decision of 12 April, Ganesh Kumaran
tabled the commercial terms for the replacement FKM seal supplier.

The vendor is **Tanaka Seals & Gaskets KK**, of Osaka, Japan. Tanaka was
shortlisted during the Q1 supply review after an audit of four candidate
suppliers and scored highest on quality system maturity and on marine
reference installations.

Terms agreed subject to contract:

| Term | Value |
|---|---|
| Lead time | 6 weeks |
| Minimum order quantity | 200 sets |
| Unit price | JPY 4,800 per set |
| Payment terms | Net 45 days |
| Late delivery penalty | 0.5% per week, capped at 5% |
| Contract term | 24 months |

The lead time improvement is the central benefit: **14 weeks currently,
falling to 6 weeks with Tanaka, a reduction of 8 weeks.** Priya Ramasamy
noted this releases roughly RM 1.4 million of work in progress and is the
main reason to accept the higher unit price.

Unit price comparison: the incumbent supplies at JPY 4,150 per set, so
Tanaka is approximately 15.7% more expensive per set. The Committee
accepted this on the basis of lead time and quality performance.

**Action:** Hafiz Zulkifli to issue contract NWH-SUP-2026-014 for legal
review, targeting an effective date of 1 June 2026. *(Owner: HZ, due
20 May)*

**Action:** Rajesh Nair to complete first-article approval before any
production use. Reconfirmed as a hard gate. *(Owner: RN, due 5 June)*

## 2. Incumbent Seal Supplier — Exit

The incumbent will be retained as a qualified second source for the
NH-8840 nitrile seal kits, where performance has been acceptable. Only
the FKM marine seal scope moves to Tanaka.

Ganesh Kumaran to give notice on the marine scope once Tanaka's
first-article is approved, not before, to avoid a supply gap.

## 3. Bronze Billet Cost

Hafiz Zulkifli reported aluminium bronze billet cost has risen 22% since
the 2026 H1 price list was published, driven by nickel. At current
volumes this adds approximately RM 980 to the material cost of each
NH-8840-X housing.

**Action:** Ganesh Kumaran to refer the bronze cost increase to the
Product Committee for a pricing decision. *(Owner: GK, due 31 May)*

## 4. Inventory

Michelle Wong reported total inventory of RM 8.9 million, against a target
of RM 7.5 million. Marine work in progress accounts for RM 2.3 million of
the excess and should unwind as seal supply normalises.

Slow-moving stock review identified RM 340,000 of NH-4420 and NH-1150
parts. Given the NH-1150 has no further spares commitment, Michelle
proposed writing down the NH-1150 residual. Priya Ramasamy agreed in
principle, subject to confirming there is no contractual spares
obligation.
