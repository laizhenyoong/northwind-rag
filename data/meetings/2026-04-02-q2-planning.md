# Q2 FY2026 Business Planning Meeting

**Date:** 2 April 2026, 09:00–12:15
**Venue:** Shah Alam, Board Room

**Present:** Datuk Lim Soo Keat (CEO), Chong Wei Ming (Head of
Engineering), Siti Zubaidah Hashim (Operations Manager), Tan Mei Ling
(Head of Sales), Ganesh Kumaran (Supply Chain Manager), Priya Ramasamy
(Finance Director), Nurul Aisyah Ibrahim (HR Manager), Rajesh Nair

## 1. Q1 Performance Review

Priya presented Q1 actuals. Revenue for the quarter was RM 10.8 million
against a plan of RM 11.5 million, a shortfall of 6.1%. The gap is
attributed almost entirely to slipped marine deliveries rather than to
order intake, which was ahead of plan.

Gross margin held at 34.2%, slightly above the 33.5% plan, helped by
favourable gear pump volumes.

## 2. FY2026 Revenue Target

The Committee **confirmed the full-year revenue target of RM 48 million**
for FY2026, unchanged from the approved plan. Tan Mei Ling noted this
requires an average of RM 12.4 million per quarter for the remaining three
quarters and depends on clearing the marine backlog.

Order book at 31 March stood at RM 21.3 million, of which RM 7.9 million
is marine product.

## 3. Marine Delivery Backlog

Siti Zubaidah reported that eleven NH-8840-X units were undelivered at
quarter end, all held at the same point in the build: awaiting seal kits.
The current seal supply lead time is unacceptable and is now the single
largest constraint on revenue recognition.

**Action:** Chong Wei Ming and Ganesh Kumaran to bring a resolution to the
engineering sync on 12 April, drawing on the vendor options identified in
the Q1 supply review. *(Owner: CWM / GK, due 12 April)*

The Chair asked that this be treated as the top operational priority for
Q2.

## 4. Headcount

Nurul Aisyah presented the headcount position at 178 against an approved
establishment of 186.

The Committee agreed a **general headcount freeze for the remainder of
FY2026**, with three exceptions already approved and now confirmed:

1. Senior Design Engineer, marine products (replacement)
2. Test Engineer, hydraulics test bay (new)
3. Applications Engineer, Pasir Gudang (new)

All other vacancies are suspended. Any further exception requires the
Chair's written approval.

## 5. NH-9000 Development Programme

Chong Wei Ming presented the NH-9000 business case, a 160 cc/rev pump for
heavy industrial duty, requiring RM 2.1 million of development spend
across 18 months.

After discussion, the Committee **deferred the NH-9000 programme to
FY2027**. The reasoning recorded was that engineering capacity in FY2026
must be directed at the marine seal problem and at closing out the NH-4420
transition, and that committing RM 2.1 million would put the FY2026 margin
target at risk.

Chong Wei Ming asked that the concept work already completed be preserved
and that the decision be revisited in the FY2027 planning round in October
2026. The Chair agreed.

## 7. Quality

Rajesh Nair reported the Q1 customer complaint rate at 1.8 per 100 units
shipped, against a target of 1.5. Six of the eleven complaints in the
quarter related to marine seal weepage. Rajesh noted the causes appear to
split between the seal batch problem and customers not observing the
flushing interval, and undertook to separate the two in Q2 reporting.
