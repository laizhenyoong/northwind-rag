## 1. NH-8840-X Seal Failures — Root Cause

Lee Chun Hoe presented the investigation into shaft seal weepage on the
NH-8840-X.

Twenty-three field returns since October 2025 were examined. The findings
split into two distinct populations:

**Population A — 9 units.** Seal hardness measured outside specification,
Shore A 82–86 against a drawing requirement of 75 ±3. All nine trace to
two FKM batches delivered in Q3 2025. This is a supplier quality failure.

**Population B — 14 units.** Seal hardness within specification, but heavy
salt crystallisation on the housing and shaft. In every case the customer
had no record of freshwater flushing. This is a maintenance
non-compliance, not a product defect, and reinforces the 500-hour flushing
requirement in the specification.

Rajesh Nair asked that the two populations be reported separately to the
Management Committee, as combining them overstates the product defect rate.

**Action:** Lee Chun Hoe to issue the failure report separating the two
populations. *(Owner: LCH, due 24 April)*

## 2. Seal Supply — Decision

The current seal supplier's quoted lead time has extended to **14 weeks**,
and the Q3 2025 hardness excursion has not been satisfactorily explained
despite two formal quality escalations.

The meeting **agreed to move NH-8840-X seal supply to the alternate vendor
identified in the Q1 supply review**, subject to first-article approval
and a commercial agreement. Ganesh Kumaran confirmed the vendor's
indicative lead time is materially better and that he holds the commercial
detail from the Q1 review.

Conditions attached to the changeover:

1. First-article inspection on three sets, including Shore A hardness at
   five points and a 720-hour salt spray test per ISO 9227.
2. Dimensional verification against drawing DWG-8840X-SEAL-02 revision D.
3. No production use before Rajesh Nair signs the first-article report.
4. Existing stock of conforming seals to be consumed first.

**Action:** Ganesh Kumaran to table full commercial terms and the vendor
qualification status at the supply chain review on 3 May.
*(Owner: GK, due 3 May)*

**Action:** Nor Azlina Rashid to reserve HTB-4 for first-article salt
spray and endurance testing in the week commencing 18 May.
*(Owner: NAR, due 15 May)*

## 4. NH-4420 Transition

Vincent Teoh reported that fourteen customers still operate the NH-4420
and have been contacted about the withdrawal. The recurring question is
whether the NH-8840-X is a drop-in replacement. It is not: the NH-4420
uses a 30 mm parallel-key shaft against the NH-8840-X's 32 mm splined
shaft, so a coupling change is always required.

**Action:** Vincent Teoh to prepare a one-page retrofit note for Sales
covering the shaft and coupling difference. *(Owner: VT, due 30 April)*
