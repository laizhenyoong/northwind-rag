# Discontinued Products and Spares Availability

This register lists withdrawn models, their replacements and the date
after which spare parts will no longer be supplied.

## 1. Pumps

| Model | Discontinued | Replacement | Spares supported until |
|---|---|---|---|
| NH-6600 | 2023-12-31 | NH-8840 | 2028-12-31 |
| NH-6620 | 2023-12-31 | NH-8860 | 2028-12-31 |
| NH-4420 | 2026-06-30 | NH-8840-X | 2031-06-30 |
| NH-3300 | 2019-03-31 | NH-8825 | exhausted, no spares |

### NH-6600

Withdrawn at the end of 2023 and replaced by the NH-8840. The NH-8840 is
dimensionally compatible on the mounting flange but the case drain moved
from 1/2 in to 3/4 in BSPP, so a retrofit requires a new drain hose.
Seal kits and bearing sets remain available until 31 December 2028.

### NH-4420

The marine predecessor to the NH-8840-X. Discontinued 30 June 2026 by
decision of the Product Committee. The NH-8840-X is the replacement but is
**not** a drop-in fit: the NH-4420 used a 30 mm parallel-key shaft, while
the NH-8840-X uses a 32 mm splined shaft. A coupling change is required.

Spares are guaranteed for five years to 30 June 2031, reflecting the
long service life of vessels in which the pump is installed.

### NH-3300

Withdrawn in 2019. Spares stock is exhausted and no further parts will be
manufactured. Customers still operating this model should be directed to
the NH-8825 with a full replacement quotation.

## 2. Valves

| Model | Discontinued | Replacement | Spares supported until |
|---|---|---|---|
| NH-1150 | 2021-09-30 | NH-2210 | exhausted, no spares |
| NH-1180 | 2021-09-30 | NH-2215 | exhausted, no spares |
| NH-2110 | 2024-12-31 | NH-2210 | 2029-12-31 |

### NH-1150

Spares are exhausted. Retrofit to the NH-2210 requires a new subplate
(SUB-NG10-A) because the NH-1150 used a proprietary interface. Quote the
valve, the subplate and two hours of fitting labour.

## 3. Accessories

| Part | Discontinued | Replacement |
|---|---|---|
| ADK-221-A | 2025-01-31 | ADK-221-M |
| FE-25-STD | 2024-06-30 | FE-10-STD |
| CB-2210-12 | 2022-12-31 | none, 12 V not supported |

The 12 V coil CB-2210-12 was withdrawn at the end of 2022. No 12 V coil is
offered on the NH-2200 series. Requests must be redirected to a 24 V
supply or a customer-supplied DC converter.

## 4. Handling Enquiries for Discontinued Models

1. Confirm the model and serial number against the ERP installed-base
   record.
2. Check this register for spares status.
3. Where spares are exhausted, raise a replacement quotation rather than
   a parts quotation.
4. Where a retrofit is involved, flag the mechanical differences noted
   above so the customer budgets for the interface work.
