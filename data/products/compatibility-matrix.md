# Pump and Valve Compatibility Matrix

Legend:

- **D** — direct fit, no additional parts
- **A:<kit>** — compatible with the stated adapter kit
- **N** — not compatible, do not quote
- **E** — engineering review required before quotation

## 1. Pump to Valve

| Pump \ Valve | NH-2210 | NH-2215 | NH-2230 | NH-2410 | NH-2510 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NH-8825 | D | D | D | D | D |
| NH-8840 | D | D | N | D | D |
| NH-8840-X | A:ADK-221-M | A:ADK-221-S | N | D | E |
| NH-8860 | D | D | N | D | D |
| NH-8860-X | A:ADK-221-M | A:ADK-221-S | N | D | E |
| NH-7710 | D | N | D | D | D |
| NH-7720 | D | D | D | D | D |

Notes:

1. The NH-2230 is an NG6 interface valve and cannot carry the flow of the
   NH-8840 family. Pairing is blocked in the quotation system.
2. Marine pumps require an adapter kit for NG10 directional valves because
   the bronze housing routes the case drain on the opposite face.
3. NH-2510 with a marine pump requires engineering review as the flow
   control body is not salt-spray qualified.

## 2. Seal Material Compatibility

| Fluid | NBR seals | FKM seals |
|---|---|---|
| Mineral oil ISO 11158 HM | Yes | Yes |
| HEES biodegradable ester | No | Yes |
| HFC water-glycol | Yes | No |
| Phosphate ester HFDR | No | Yes |
| Seawater splash exposure | No | Yes |

The NH-8840 ships with NBR as standard and is therefore not suitable for
HEES or phosphate ester service without a seal change.

## 3. Filter Element Cross-Reference

| Pump | Required element | Micron |
|---|---|---|
| NH-8825 | FE-10-STD | 10 |
| NH-8840 | FE-10-STD | 10 |
| NH-8840-X | FE-06-MAR | 6 |
| NH-8860 | FE-10-STD | 10 |
| NH-8860-X | FE-06-MAR | 6 |
| NH-7710 | FE-10-STD | 10 |
| NH-7720 | FE-10-STD | 10 |

Fitting a 10 µm element to a marine pump is a warranty exclusion.

## 4. Subplate Cross-Reference

| Valve | Subplate | Interface |
|---|---|---|
| NH-2210 | SUB-NG10-A | ISO 4401-05-04-0-05 |
| NH-2215 | SUB-NG10-A | ISO 4401-05-04-0-05 |
| NH-2230 | SUB-NG6-A | ISO 4401-03-03-0-05 |
| NH-1150 (obsolete) | SUB-NG10-OLD | proprietary |

The obsolete NH-1150 subplate is not compatible with any current valve. A
retrofit to NH-2210 requires replacement of the subplate.
