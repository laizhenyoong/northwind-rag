# TKT-10237 — Warranty claim declined, out of period

## Customer report

Bearing failure on the NH-8840 driving the sterilizer hoist circuit.
Customer submitted a warranty claim for a free-of-charge replacement,
stating the pump "should still be covered".

## Warranty assessment

| Check | Value |
|---|---|
| Delivery date | 2023-05-10 |
| Claim date | 2026-05-06 |
| Elapsed since delivery | 35 months |
| Operating hours at failure | 9,412 |
| Applicable warranty | Standard industrial |

The standard industrial warranty runs for 18 months from delivery or
4,000 operating hours, whichever occurs first. Both limits are exceeded by
a wide margin, the hours limit by more than double.

**Claim declined.** Explained to the customer in writing with the
delivery date and hour meter reading set out.

## Technical findings

- Front bearing outer race spalled over approximately 30% of the
  circumference.
- Wear pattern consistent with normal end-of-life fatigue, not with
  contamination or misalignment.
- Fluid analysis from January 2026 was within specification, so the
  customer's fluid maintenance was not a contributing factor.

At 9,412 hours the unit was approaching the 12,000-hour overhaul point.
The failure is consistent with normal service life rather than a defect.

## Commercial outcome

Chargeable overhaul quoted using overhaul kit SP-8840-OH at RM 2,850 plus
workshop labour, presented as better value than a new pump at RM 12,400.

Customer accepted the overhaul. Unit collected 2026-05-12 and booked into
Shah Alam.

## Note

Customer's maintenance planner was unaware that the warranty carries an
operating-hours limit as well as a time limit. Account manager to walk
through the warranty clause at the next site visit. Marine product carries
different limits, which added to the confusion as this customer operates
both industrial and marine units.
