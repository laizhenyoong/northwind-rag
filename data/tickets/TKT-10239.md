# TKT-10239 — Coil burnt out on commissioning

## Customer report

Valve energised for the first time during commissioning of a pipe bending
press. Smoke from the solenoid within approximately three minutes.
Customer reported the valve as dead on arrival and requested warranty
replacement.

## Findings

- Coil open circuit. Winding insulation charred and the encapsulation
  discoloured and swollen.
- Order code `NH-2210-24-E-N` confirmed on the body label. Coil is 24 V DC,
  30 W.
- Control panel supply measured at **48 V DC**. The panel was built by a
  third-party integrator and fed the solenoid from an unregulated 48 V
  battery-backed rail.
- Spool free and undamaged. Body and seals unaffected.

## Root cause

Sustained overvoltage. At 48 V the coil dissipates approximately four times
its rated power, and the winding fails within minutes. The coil is rated
24 V DC with a tolerance of ±10%, so the maximum permissible steady
voltage is 26.4 V.

Not a manufacturing defect. **Warranty declined.**

## Resolution

1. Replacement coil CB-2210-24 supplied at RM 310, chargeable.
2. Integrator advised in writing to provide a regulated 24 V DC supply for
   the solenoid circuit.
3. Customer's electrician confirmed a 48 V to 24 V DC-DC converter fitted
   before the replacement coil was energised.
4. Valve function verified remotely by the customer on 2026-06-12.

## Note

Third voltage-related NH-2210 incident in FY2026, following one
under-voltage case at 12 V and one earlier over-voltage case. All three
involved third-party panel builders. Reinforces the request to make coil
voltage an explicit sign-off line on the commissioning checklist.
