# TKT-10238 — Transit damage on delivery

## Customer report

Unit received at Vung Tau. On unpacking, the suction flange was visibly
bent and the timber crate was split along one side. Customer photographed
the crate before opening and refused to install the pump.

## Assessment

Photographs reviewed same day. Findings:

- Crate side panel split with a puncture consistent with forklift tine
  contact.
- Suction flange bent approximately 6 mm out of plane. Flange face no
  longer sealable.
- Case drain port thread undamaged.
- Shaft rotated freely by hand with no roughness, suggesting the rotating
  group was not affected, but this could not be confirmed without a bench
  test.

Despatch records show the unit passed bench test and certification at Shah
Alam on 2026-04-14 with no anomalies, and photographs taken at packing show
an undamaged flange. Damage therefore occurred in transit.

## Resolution

1. Replacement NH-8860 built from stock and air freighted, arriving
   2026-05-08.
2. Damaged unit returned to Shah Alam for assessment. Bench test on return
   confirmed the rotating group was undamaged; flange replaced and the unit
   returned to saleable stock as a refurbished item.
3. Insurance claim lodged against the freight forwarder for the air freight
   cost and the flange repair. Claim reference INS-2026-0044.

## Commercial

No charge to customer. Air freight of RM 8,400 recovered under the carrier
claim, settled 2026-06-30.

Terms on the original shipment were FOB Port Klang, so title had passed to
the customer at the port. The Company nonetheless replaced the unit
immediately to protect the customer's project schedule, and pursued the
carrier claim jointly with the customer's insurer.

## Note

Second transit damage incident on the Vung Tau route in FY2026. Supply
chain to review crate specification for this lane and consider steel corner
protection.
