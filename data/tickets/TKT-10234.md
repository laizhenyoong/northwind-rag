# TKT-10234 — NH-8840-X will not mount to NH-2210

## Customer report

Fitters attempting to assemble a new steering gear power pack. The
NH-8840-X pump and NH-2210 valve will not mate. Customer believes parts
are missing from the shipment and the build is now holding a vessel
delivery.

## Diagnosis (2026-05-12, same day, by photograph)

Photographs confirmed the pump is an NH-8840-X with the bronze housing, in
which the case drain is routed on the opposite face compared with the
standard NH-8840. The NH-2210 cannot be mounted directly to a marine
housing for this reason.

Adapter kit **ADK-221-M** is mandatory for this combination. Checking the
sales order, the adapter kit was not included on the order and had not
been quoted.

## Root cause

Order omission at quotation stage. Not a shipment shortage and not a
product defect. The compatibility requirement is documented, but the
quotation did not carry the kit.

## Resolution

1. ADK-221-M despatched same day by courier from Shah Alam stock,
   supplied free of charge as a commercial gesture given the vessel
   delivery risk.
2. Fitting instructions issued by email; kit installed by the customer
   without a service visit.
3. Assembly completed 2026-05-14 and confirmed by the customer.

## Commercial

Kit value RM 1,240 written off to sales goodwill. Approved by Tan Mei Ling.

## Prevention

This is the fifth order in FY2026 where the adapter kit was omitted for a
marine pump paired with an NG10 directional valve. Referred to the Product
Committee to have the quotation system add the kit automatically.
