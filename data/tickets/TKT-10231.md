# TKT-10231 — NH-8840 running hot

## Customer report

Pump on billet transfer line running hot. Case drain measured 96 °C with a
handheld probe. Operators report a burning smell after about two hours of
continuous running. Line stopped as a precaution.

## Site findings (Pasir Gudang service centre, 2026-03-06)

- Reservoir fluid identified as ISO VG 15 mineral oil, not the specified
  grade.
- Fluid sample viscosity measured **9.4 cSt at operating temperature**,
  below the specified minimum of 12 cSt for this pump.
- Fluid analysis returned ISO 4406 21/19/15, well outside the required
  18/16/13.
- Return filter element last changed at an unknown date; differential
  indicator in bypass.
- No mechanical damage to the rotating group. Bearings and swash plate
  within wear limits.

## Root cause

Operating outside the permitted viscosity range. Low viscosity reduced the
hydrodynamic film in the piston-barrel interface, increasing internal
leakage and generating heat. Contaminated fluid accelerated the effect.

Not a product defect. Warranty claim declined and recorded as an
application issue.

## Actions taken

1. System flushed and refilled with ISO VG 46 mineral oil to ISO 11158 HM.
2. Return filter element FE-10-STD replaced.
3. Operating temperature after correction: case drain 61 °C at full load.
4. Customer maintenance staff briefed on the viscosity window and on the
   1,000-hour fluid sampling interval.

## Follow-up

Vincent Teoh to include the viscosity window on the commissioning
checklist. Customer has agreed to a fluid analysis contract.
