# TKT-10240 — NH-1150 spares request

## Customer report

Spool seizing intermittently on a decoiler circuit. Customer requested a
spool assembly and seal kit for the NH-1150.

## Position advised

The NH-1150 was discontinued on 30 September 2021. **Spares stock is
exhausted and no further parts will be manufactured.** No spool assembly
or seal kit can be supplied.

The replacement valve is the NH-2210. The retrofit is not a straight swap:
the NH-1150 used a proprietary subplate interface, while the NH-2210 uses
ISO 4401-05-04-0-05 (NG10). A new subplate, SUB-NG10-A, is required.

## Retrofit quotation raised

| Item | Qty | Unit price |
|---|---|---|
| NH-2210-24-E-N directional control valve | 1 | RM 3,150 |
| SUB-NG10-A subplate | 1 | quoted separately |
| Fitting labour, 2 hours | 1 | included in call-out |
| Field service call-out, standard | 1 | RM 750 |

Advised the customer that the existing NH-1150 subplate must be removed
and the pipework re-terminated to the new subplate, and that this is the
main labour element.

## Customer response

Customer initially pushed back, having purchased the valve in 2016 and
expecting parts availability. Explained that the model has been out of
production for over four years and that the spares commitment expired.

Customer noted they have three further NH-1150 valves on site. Advised
them to plan a phased replacement rather than wait for the next failure,
since no emergency parts route exists.

## Outcome

Retrofit order received 2026-04-07 for one valve, with a follow-up enquiry
for the remaining three units to be budgeted in their FY2027 capital plan.

Account manager to prepare a three-valve replacement proposal.
