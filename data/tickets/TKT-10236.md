# TKT-10236 — NH-8840 excessive noise

## Customer report

Safety officer at the customer's site raised a noise complaint after a
workplace survey measured 83 dB(A) at one metre from the power unit. The
datasheet states 74 dB(A). Customer asked whether the pump was
out of specification and whether they had grounds for a claim.

## Findings (2026-01-16)

- Noise character was a distinct rattle rising with load, consistent with
  cavitation rather than mechanical wear.
- Suction strainer heavily contaminated, estimated 70% blocked.
- Suction line vacuum measured 0.42 bar absolute at the pump inlet,
  against a permitted minimum of 0.8 bar absolute.
- Reservoir fluid level 40 mm below the low mark.
- Rotating group inspected through the drain port; no scoring visible.
- Speed confirmed at 1,480 rpm, within the 600–2400 rpm range.

## Root cause

Inlet starvation caused by a blocked suction strainer and low fluid level,
producing cavitation. Cavitation noise is broadband and adds roughly 8 to
10 dB(A) over normal running noise.

The pump itself was within specification. The measurement condition also
differed from the datasheet basis, which is 1500 rpm and 250 bar per
ISO 4412-1; the customer's survey was taken at peak load.

## Work done

1. Suction strainer cleaned and refitted.
2. Reservoir topped to correct level with matched fluid.
3. Suction line vacuum after correction: 0.86 bar absolute.
4. Noise re-measured at 1 m: **75 dB(A)** at datasheet conditions,
   78 dB(A) at peak load.

## Outcome

Customer accepted the finding. No warranty claim pursued.

Advised that the suction strainer is not on the published service schedule
because it is a customer-supplied reservoir item, and recommended a
3-monthly inspection be added to their own maintenance plan.
