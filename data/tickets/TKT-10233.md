# TKT-10233 — NH-2210 not shifting fully

## Customer report

Newly commissioned press circuit at the Lahad Datu mill. Cylinder extends
slowly and stalls. Customer's electrician reports the solenoid "buzzes but
does not click" and suspects a faulty valve. Requested replacement under
warranty.

## Remote diagnosis (2026-04-21, telephone)

Applications engineer asked the customer to measure the voltage at the DIN
connector while the solenoid was commanded on.

Measured: **12.4 V DC**.

Order code on the valve confirmed as `NH-2210-24-E-N`. The coil is a 24 V
DC unit.

## Root cause

Wiring error at commissioning. The valve was connected to a 12 V control
supply instead of 24 V. At half the nominal voltage the solenoid develops
insufficient force to complete the spool stroke, so the valve partially
shifts and metering is restricted. This presents as a slow or stalling
actuator and is frequently misreported as a valve defect.

No fault with the valve.

## Resolution

1. Customer's electrician re-terminated the solenoid to the 24 V DC rail.
2. Valve shifted correctly on first command. Switching time verified by
   ear and by cylinder response.
3. Coil resistance checked and found nominal; no thermal damage from the
   under-voltage condition.
4. No parts supplied. No charge raised.

## Notes

The customer had asked at order stage whether a 12 V coil was available.
It is not; no 12 V coil is offered on the NH-2200 series and the previous
12 V part was withdrawn some years ago. Where a customer has only a 12 V
control system, a DC-DC converter must be supplied by the customer.

Recommend the coil voltage be added as an explicit confirmation line on
the commissioning checklist. Raised with Vincent Teoh.
