# TKT-10235 — NH-6600 spares enquiry

## Customer report

Maintenance planner asked whether the NH-6600 is still supported and
whether a seal kit and bearing set can be supplied. Pump is on a plate
rolling line, installed 2019, approaching 19,000 operating hours.

The customer had heard "from a colleague" that the model was obsolete and
was concerned they would have to replace the whole power unit.

## Response provided

The NH-6600 was discontinued on 31 December 2023 and is no longer
manufactured. However **spare parts remain available until 31 December
2028**, so there is no immediate need to replace the unit.

The replacement model, should the customer choose to change, is the
NH-8840. The mounting flange is dimensionally compatible, but the case
drain moved from 1/2 in to 3/4 in BSPP, so a retrofit requires a new drain
hose. This was explained so the customer can budget correctly if they
decide to upgrade later.

## Quotation raised

| Item | Qty | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Seal kit, NH-6600 | 1 | Ex stock |
| Bearing set, NH-6600 | 1 | 6 weeks |
| Overhaul labour, workshop | 1 | Pasir Gudang |

Customer advised that at 19,000 hours the unit is past the 12,000-hour
overhaul point for the family and that a workshop overhaul is the better
value option compared with piecemeal parts.

## Outcome

Customer accepted the overhaul quotation. Unit scheduled into Pasir Gudang
for August 2026 during their planned shutdown.

## Note

Third NH-6600 enquiry this quarter where the customer believed the model
was entirely unsupported. Suggest Sales issue a short bulletin confirming
the 2028 spares date to all NH-6600 operators.
