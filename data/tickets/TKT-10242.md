# TKT-10242 — Reduced delivery on NH-8840

## Customer report

Plate roll drive cycle times have lengthened by roughly 18% over the past
year. Customer suspected the pump and asked for an assessment before
committing to a replacement.

## Findings (on site, 2026-06-25)

- Measured delivery **118 L/min** at 250 bar, against a rated maximum of
  145 L/min. A loss of approximately 19%.
- Case drain flow measured 6.8 L/min, against a typical new-unit figure of
  1.9 L/min. Elevated case drain flow indicates internal leakage past the
  pistons and valve plate.
- Volumetric efficiency calculated at 81%, against 94% when new.
- Hour meter: **11,840 operating hours.**
- Fluid analysis clean at ISO 4406 17/15/12. Filter changes documented and
  on schedule.
- No external leakage, no abnormal noise, temperature normal at 58 °C case
  drain.

## Assessment

Normal internal wear consistent with accumulated hours. At 11,840 hours the
unit is at the published 12,000-hour overhaul point for this family. The
customer's fluid maintenance has been good, which is why the unit has
reached the interval without earlier trouble.

No defect. No warranty consideration, and none was sought.

## Recommendation and outcome

Workshop overhaul using kit SP-8840-OH at RM 2,850 plus labour, against a
new unit at RM 12,400. Overhaul restores volumetric efficiency to within
2% of a new unit and resets the interval.

Customer accepted. Unit exchanged for a workshop float unit on 2026-06-30
to avoid downtime, with the customer's own unit overhauled and returned to
their stores as the next float.

## Note

Good example for the Sales team of a well-maintained unit reaching its
published overhaul interval predictably. Customer's fluid analysis contract
is the reason. Worth using as a reference when selling fluid analysis to
other accounts.
