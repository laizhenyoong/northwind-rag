# TKT-10232 — NH-8840-X shaft seal weepage

## Customer report

Oil weepage from the shaft seal area on the deck crane power pack of MV
Selat Mas. Approximately 200 ml lost per day. Vessel alongside at Pasir
Gudang. Customer requested warranty repair.

## Findings (on board, 2026-02-21)

- Heavy salt crystallisation on the housing face and around the shaft seal
  lip. Deposits approximately 2 mm thick in places.
- Shaft seal lip hardened and cracked circumferentially.
- Seal hardness on the removed part measured Shore A 76, **within** the
  drawing requirement of 75 ±3. The seal was therefore conforming.
- Bronze housing showed pitting consistent with prolonged salt exposure.
- Unit had accumulated 3,120 operating hours since commissioning.

## Flushing records

Customer was asked for freshwater flushing records. **No flushing records
exist for this unit.** The chief engineer confirmed that flushing had not
been carried out at any point since installation in March 2025.

The specification requires a freshwater flush of the housing and shaft seal
area every 500 operating hours in seawater-exposed service. At 3,120 hours
the unit should have received six flushes.

## Root cause

Salt ingress and crystallisation at the shaft seal, caused by failure to
observe the freshwater flushing interval. The seal itself was conforming.

## Warranty position

Warranty on the shaft seal assembly is void where the flushing interval is
not observed. **Claim declined.** Repair carried out as a chargeable job.

## Work done

1. Shaft seal assembly replaced, seal kit SK-8840-V.
2. Housing face cleaned and inspected; pitting within acceptable limits, no
   housing replacement required.
3. Freshwater flush performed and procedure SB-2024-11 demonstrated to the
   vessel's engineers.
4. Flushing log sheet left on board and flushing added to the vessel's
   planned maintenance system.

## Commercial

Chargeable: seal kit RM 780, field service call-out RM 750, labour 6 hours.
Customer accepted the charge after the flushing evidence was presented.

## Note

This is the fourth unit from this customer with the same pattern. Account
manager to raise flushing compliance at the next quarterly review.
