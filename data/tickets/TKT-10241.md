# TKT-10241 — Contamination damage on NH-8840-X

## Customer report

Falling delivery and rising noise on the mooring winch power pack of MV
Selat Johor. Customer reported a suspected internal fault and claimed
under warranty. Unit had 2,240 operating hours.

## Findings (workshop strip, Pasir Gudang, 2026-02-03)

- Valve plate scored radially, depth up to 0.08 mm.
- Piston shoes showed bronze transfer and three shoes had begun to lift.
- Fluid sample returned ISO 4406 **20/18/14**, against the required
  17/15/12 for this model.
- Return filter element removed and identified as **FE-10-STD**, a 10 µm
  element. This pump requires **FE-06-MAR**, a 6 µm element.
- Element housing is common to both parts, so the wrong element fits
  physically without any obvious sign of error.

## Root cause

Insufficient filtration. The marine pump requires 6 µm nominal filtration
owing to its tighter internal clearances and the higher cleanliness
requirement. A 10 µm element allows particles to pass that the valve plate
and piston shoes cannot tolerate, producing the observed three-body
abrasive wear.

Fitting a 10 µm element to a marine pump is an express warranty exclusion.
**Claim declined.**

## How the error arose

The customer's storeman ordered by description, "filter element, return
line", and received the standard part. Both elements were held in the same
bin location on the vessel. The customer accepted the finding once the two
part numbers were shown side by side.

## Work done

1. Valve plate and piston shoe set replaced; overhaul kit SP-8840X-OH.
2. Housing and shaft seal inspected; no salt damage, flushing records for
   this unit were complete and in order.
3. System flushed to ISO 4406 16/14/11 before recommissioning.
4. Correct FE-06-MAR elements supplied, six units, and the vessel's spares
   bin relabelled.

## Commercial

Chargeable: overhaul kit RM 4,600, elements 6 × RM 245, workshop labour
18 hours. Total accepted by the customer.

## Prevention

Recommended that marine and industrial elements be held in separate,
clearly labelled locations, and that the vessel's planned maintenance
system record the part number rather than a description. Filter
cross-reference table forwarded to the customer's technical superintendent.
