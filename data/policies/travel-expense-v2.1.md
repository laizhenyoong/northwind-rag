# Employee Travel and Expense Policy (v2.1)

This version replaces version 1.4 with effect from **1 March 2026**.
Claims for travel completed before that date remain governed by v1.4.

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out the rules for booking, incurring and claiming
business travel expenses at Northwind Hydraulics Sdn Bhd ("the Company").
It applies to all permanent and contract staff.

## 2. Per Diem Allowances

Per diem covers meals and incidental expenses. Receipts are not required
for per diem claims.

| Travel type | Rate per day |
|---|---|
| Domestic (within Malaysia) | RM 180 |
| International (general) | RM 420 |
| Singapore and Thailand | RM 300 |

Per diem is claimable for each full day away from the employee's normal
place of work. Partial days of less than 6 hours are not claimable.

Where a meal is provided by a customer, supplier or conference host, the
per diem for that day is reduced by RM 40 per meal provided.

## 3. Approval Thresholds

| Total claim value | Required approval | Form |
|---|---|---|
| Below RM 500 | Line manager | FIN-EXP-22 |
| RM 500 and above | Finance Director | FIN-EXP-22 |

Form FIN-EXP-19 was retired on 29 February 2026 and is no longer accepted.

All claims must be submitted within 30 days of the last day of travel.
Claims submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed.

## 4. Air Travel

Economy class is the default for all staff. **Business class may be
approved for flights with a scheduled duration exceeding 7 hours, and
only for staff at Director grade and above.** Such bookings require
written pre-approval from the Finance Director.

Bookings must be made through the appointed travel agent at least 14 days
before departure where practicable. Bookings made inside 7 days require a
justification note.

## 5. Accommodation

Hotel accommodation is capped at **RM 320 per night** for domestic travel
and **RM 700 per night** for international travel. Staff must select the
lowest reasonable rate available.

Where a customer site visit requires accommodation above these caps, the
Supply Chain Manager or Head of Engineering may endorse an exception of
up to 25% above the cap.

## 6. Ground Transport and Mileage

Use of a personal vehicle for business travel is reimbursed at
**RM 0.70 per kilometre**. Toll and parking receipts are claimable in
addition to mileage.

Taxi and e-hailing fares are claimable with receipts. Journeys above
RM 120 require a written justification in the claim notes.

## 7. Non-Claimable Items

The following are never reimbursable:

- Alcoholic beverages
- In-room entertainment and minibar
- Traffic or parking fines
- Personal travel insurance beyond the corporate policy
- Costs for accompanying family members
- Airline seat selection and priority boarding fees

## 8. Foreign Currency

Claims in foreign currency are converted at the Bank Negara Malaysia
mid-rate on the last day of travel. Credit card statements showing the
actual converted amount may be submitted instead.

## 9. Corporate Card

Staff issued a corporate card must reconcile transactions within 15 days
of statement date. Unreconciled balances above RM 2,000 will result in
temporary card suspension.

## 10. Breaches

Deliberate misstatement of an expense claim is treated as gross
misconduct under the Employee Handbook and may result in dismissal.
