# Remote and Hybrid Working Policy

## 1. Scope

This policy governs work performed away from a Northwind Hydraulics site.
It applies to the Shah Alam head office and the Pasir Gudang service
centre.

## 2. Eligibility

Remote working is available to staff who have completed **6 months of
continuous service** and whose role does not require physical presence.

The following roles are **not eligible** for remote working under any
arrangement:

- Production floor operators and line leads
- Warehouse and goods-inward staff
- QA inspection staff
- Field service engineers while on assignment
- Facilities and security personnel

Eligibility does not create an entitlement. Approval remains at the
discretion of the Head of Department.

## 3. Permitted Pattern

Eligible staff may work remotely a **maximum of 2 days per week**. Days
must be fixed in advance and recorded in the team calendar. Ad hoc
substitution of days requires manager agreement by the preceding working
day.

Fridays are designated an all-hands on-site day for Engineering during
active design review cycles.

## 4. Core Hours

Staff working remotely must be reachable during **core hours of 10:00 to
16:00 MYT** and must respond to messages within 60 minutes during that
window. Time outside core hours may be worked flexibly provided
deliverables and meeting commitments are met.

## 5. Application

Apply using form **HR-RW-11**, countersigned by the Head of Department
and lodged with HR. Arrangements are reviewed every 12 months and may be
withdrawn with 4 weeks' notice.

## 6. Equipment and Allowance

A one-time home office equipment allowance of **RM 800** is available to
approved remote workers, claimable against receipts within 90 days of
approval. The allowance is not repeated if an arrangement lapses and is
later renewed.

The Company provides a laptop. Staff must not process Company data on
personal devices. Company data must not be stored in personal cloud
accounts.

## 7. Information Security

Remote workers must use the Company VPN for access to the ERP, the
engineering file server and the CAD vault. Public or shared Wi-Fi
requires VPN before any Company system is accessed. Printing of documents
classified Confidential or Restricted outside a Company site is
prohibited.

## 8. Health and Safety

Staff are responsible for a safe home working environment. The Company
may request a self-assessment checklist. Work-related injury sustained
while remote working must be reported to the Safety Officer within 24
hours.

## 9. Cross-Border Remote Work

Working from outside Malaysia is not permitted without prior written
approval from the CEO, owing to tax and permanent-establishment risk.
Requests must be submitted at least 8 weeks in advance.
