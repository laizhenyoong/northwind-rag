# Employee Travel and Expense Policy (v1.4)

> **NOTICE:** This version was superseded on 1 March 2026 by version 2.1.
> It is retained for audit purposes only. Do not use for new claims.

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out the rules for booking, incurring and claiming
business travel expenses at Northwind Hydraulics Sdn Bhd ("the Company").
It applies to all permanent and contract staff.

## 2. Per Diem Allowances

Per diem covers meals and incidental expenses. Receipts are not required
for per diem claims.

| Travel type | Rate per day |
|---|---|
| Domestic (within Malaysia) | RM 150 |
| International (general) | RM 380 |
| Singapore and Thailand | RM 280 |

Per diem is claimable for each full day away from the employee's normal
place of work. Partial days of less than 6 hours are not claimable.

## 3. Approval Thresholds

| Total claim value | Required approval | Form |
|---|---|---|
| Below RM 300 | Line manager | FIN-EXP-19 |
| RM 300 and above | Finance Manager | FIN-EXP-19 |

All claims must be submitted within 30 days of the last day of travel.
Claims submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed.

## 4. Air Travel

All staff, regardless of grade, must travel economy class. Bookings must
be made through the appointed travel agent at least 14 days before
departure where practicable.

## 5. Accommodation

Hotel accommodation is capped at RM 250 per night for domestic travel and
RM 600 per night for international travel. Staff must select the lowest
reasonable rate available.

## 6. Ground Transport and Mileage

Use of a personal vehicle for business travel is reimbursed at
**RM 0.60 per kilometre**. Toll and parking receipts are claimable in
addition to mileage.

Taxi and e-hailing fares are claimable with receipts. Journeys above
RM 100 require a written justification in the claim notes.

## 7. Non-Claimable Items

The following are never reimbursable:

- Alcoholic beverages
- In-room entertainment and minibar
- Traffic or parking fines
- Personal travel insurance beyond the corporate policy
- Costs for accompanying family members

## 8. Foreign Currency

Claims in foreign currency are converted at the Bank Negara Malaysia
mid-rate on the last day of travel. Credit card statements showing the
actual converted amount may be submitted instead.

## 9. Breaches

Deliberate misstatement of an expense claim is treated as gross
misconduct under the Employee Handbook and may result in dismissal.
