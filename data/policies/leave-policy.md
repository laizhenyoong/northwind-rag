# Leave Policy

## 1. Annual Leave Entitlement

Annual leave is granted on a calendar-year basis and accrues monthly.

| Completed years of service | Annual leave days |
|---|---|
| Less than 2 years | 14 |
| 2 years to less than 5 years | 18 |
| 5 years and above | 21 |

Staff who join part way through a year receive a pro-rated entitlement
rounded up to the nearest half day.

### 1.1 Carry Forward

A maximum of **5 unused annual leave days** may be carried into the
following year. Carried days expire on **31 March** and are not
encashable after that date.

### 1.2 Application

Annual leave must be applied for using form **HR-LV-05** at least 3
working days in advance for absences of 1–2 days, and 10 working days in
advance for absences of 3 days or more. Approval rests with the line
manager, who must consider production and shipment schedules.

## 2. Medical Leave

Staff are entitled to **14 days of paid medical leave** per calendar year.

A medical certificate from a registered practitioner is required for any
absence of **2 or more consecutive days**. For single-day absences, a
same-day notification to the line manager before 09:30 is sufficient,
though the Company may request a certificate where a pattern of absence
emerges.

Hospitalisation leave of up to 60 days per year is available and is
counted separately from ordinary medical leave.

## 3. Maternity and Paternity Leave

- **Maternity leave:** 98 consecutive days of paid leave, in line with the
  Employment Act. Staff should notify HR at least 60 days before the
  expected date of confinement.
- **Paternity leave:** 7 consecutive days of paid leave, to be taken
  within 30 days of the birth. Limited to 5 confinements.

## 4. Compassionate Leave

Three days of paid leave for the death of an immediate family member
(spouse, child, parent, sibling). One day for grandparents, parents-in-law
and siblings-in-law.

## 5. Marriage Leave

Three consecutive days of paid leave, claimable once during employment,
subject to confirmation of employment.

## 6. Unpaid Leave

Unpaid leave may be granted at the Company's discretion once all annual
leave is exhausted. It requires the approval of **both** the Head of
Department and the HR Manager, and is capped at **30 calendar days** in
any rolling 12-month period. Continuous unpaid leave beyond 14 days
suspends EPF and SOCSO contributions for the period.

## 7. Public Holidays

Staff observe the gazetted public holidays for Selangor. Production staff
required to work on a public holiday receive replacement leave plus the
statutory holiday rate.

## 8. Leave During Notice Period

Annual leave may not be applied during a resignation notice period except
with the written agreement of the HR Manager. Unused balance is paid out
in the final salary.

## 9. Records

The authoritative leave balance is the figure shown in the HR system on
the first of each month. Discrepancies must be raised with HR within 14
days.
